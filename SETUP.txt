MUNCH MACHA
Working brand: Your gang. Your cravings. We deliver.
Initial location: Bengaluru. Exact delivery coverage must be confirmed.

OPEN LOCALLY
Open index.html in a browser. No build or installation is required.
Food photos are bundled in assets/; keep that folder intact when uploading.
Photo sources and licence link are in assets/PHOTO-CREDITS.txt.
The original illustration is retained as an unused asset.
Optional Google Fonts load online; system fonts work without them.

WHAT WORKS
Menu category filters, add/remove favourites, shortlist, Sunday enquiry preset,
advance-date validation, guest count validation and copyable enquiry text.
No prices, stock, availability or delivery promise is invented.

WHATSAPP CONFIGURATION BEFORE LAUNCH
Edit config.js and replace the empty whatsappNumber with your confirmed
business number: country code + number, digits only. Do not use the dummy
number in tests. This file is public; never store passwords or API secrets.
WhatsApp sending stays disabled until a valid-format number is provided.
Customers must review the message and press Send in WhatsApp themselves.
Opening WhatsApp is not confirmation of receipt, booking or payment.

HOSTING
Upload index.html, style.css, config.js, order-core.js, script.js and the assets
folder together to your chosen static host. Preserve spelling and folder case.
This package contains no backend, customer database, payment processing,
automatic messages or order tracking. Do not publish customer information in Git.
GitHub can maintain the source. Verify the host's current commercial-use policy
before using a live business storefront; a repository is not an order database.

BEFORE ACCEPTING REAL ORDERS
User supplied photos were added; confirm publication rights for all of them.
See assets/USER-PHOTOS.txt. The Adobe Stock watermarked photo was excluded.
Confirm the business number and branding, actual menu and portions, pricing,
delivery coverage/fees, preparation capacity, order cutoffs, payment and
cancellation terms, allergen handling and applicable business/food requirements.
Brand and domain availability have not been checked. No certification or
allergen-free, organic or chemical-free claims are made.
